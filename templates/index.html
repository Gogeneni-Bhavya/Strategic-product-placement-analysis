About Strategic Product Placement Analysis

Strategic Product Placement Analysis uses retail data visualization to identify effective product positioning and understand customer purchasing patterns.

  • Analyze sales volume across different product categories.
  • Evaluate product placement, consumer demographics and foot traffic.
  • Generate interactive Tableau insights for strategic retail decision-making.

The project helps retailers compare product price, competitor price, seasonal sales, promotions and store positioning to identify opportunities for improving product visibility and sales performance.
